# Hypotheses

Copy this file to `hypotheses/hypotheses.md` and fill it in.

**How we test them:** not by asking. People talk about their day, their process, the problem and the space, and we listen for whether what they describe confirms or contradicts a hypothesis. See [docs/INTERVIEWING.md](../docs/INTERVIEWING.md).

Each hypothesis has a stable number. Never renumber and never reuse a number. To drop one, set its status to `dropped` and leave the row. Interviews refer to hypotheses by number, so "what evidence do we have for H2?" is a search for `H2`.

## Status

- `open`: not enough evidence yet.
- `supported`: several stories confirm it.
- `contradicted`: several stories go against it.
- `dropped`: no longer worth testing. Say why in the evidence column.

## Canvas: the nine buckets

Each hypothesis sits in one bucket of the business model canvas. Use the bucket name exactly as written, so the hub can count them.

| Bucket | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| `customer-segments` | Who has the problem, and who are we building for? |
| `value-propositions` | What do we do for them that they value? |
| `channels` | How do we reach them and deliver it? |
| `customer-relationships` | What relationship do they expect with us? |
| `revenue-streams` | Who pays, for what, and how? |
| `key-resources` | What must we have to deliver it? |
| `key-activities` | What must we do well? |
| `key-partners` | Who do we depend on? |
| `cost-structure` | What does it cost to run? |

A healthy table has hypotheses in most buckets. If they all sit in one or two, you are only testing part of the business.
